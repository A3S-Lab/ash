# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Protect private and security-sensitive information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone is participating on behalf of `ash` or A3S Lab.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to an A3S Lab maintainer through GitHub. Do
not disclose another person's private information in a public issue.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation,
or take other appropriate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical, and enforcement decisions will consider both impact and context.
